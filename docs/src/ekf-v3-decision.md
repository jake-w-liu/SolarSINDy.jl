# EKF extension decision

## Decision

**NOT PROMOTABLE.** Retire adaptive-EKF-on-SINDy from the operational product;
retain it only as a reproducible research extension. Neither tested overlay met the
promotion gate on the revised 20-candidate, 11-active-term SINDy core. The live
monitor, API, dashboard, and alerting path therefore serve V2.1 without an EKF.

## Comparison

Both experiments use the current V2.1 discovery artifacts and calibration
features. They alter selected coefficients online but leave the sparse library
fixed. The comparison is deliberately made against the stronger of the V2.1
frozen-tail ablation and persistence, because these EKF experiments replace the
operational tail rather than reproduce the served V2.1 composition.

The causal replay covers seven G4/G5 storms from 2023–2025, with 457 scored rows
at each lead and 51 rapid-main-phase rows. Each filter is bootstrapped only on
OMNI data before the storm year. The fixed-coefficient EKF reproduces the
frozen-tail reference exactly at every lead (maximum gap 0.00 nT), which isolates
the effect of coefficient adaptation.

| overlay | lead [h] | frozen-tail RMSE [nT] | EKF RMSE [nT] | persistence RMSE [nT] |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| constrained decay adaptation | 1 | 15.95 | 16.10 | 17.80 |
| constrained decay adaptation | 2 | 25.94 | 29.17 | 29.36 |
| constrained decay adaptation | 3 | 33.93 | 39.78 | 38.75 |
| constrained decay adaptation | 6 | 54.01 | 63.57 | 63.44 |
| decay + injection adaptation | 1 | 15.95 | 16.57 | 17.80 |
| decay + injection adaptation | 2 | 25.94 | 28.42 | 29.36 |
| decay + injection adaptation | 3 | 33.93 | 38.37 | 38.75 |
| decay + injection adaptation | 6 | 54.01 | 63.08 | 63.44 |

At no lead or regime did either overlay beat the stronger comparator with a
storm-cluster 95% confidence interval strictly above zero. The injection result
was also stable across the tested process-noise sweep. Long-lead performance
worsened materially for both overlays.

## Interpretation

The current equation contains both a restoring `Dst_star` term and a southward
injection `Bs` term. The replay shows that adapting the restoring coefficient is
nearly neutral at one hour and harmful as free-running error accumulates.
Allowing the injection coefficient to move does not repair the loss. This is an
empirical result for the tested filters and constraints, not a claim that every
possible assimilation design must fail.

The package retains the EKF primitives and replay scripts for reproducibility.
They are experimental extensions of the same revised SINDy core, not an older
21/10 method and not a separate discovery model. Their scored reports are
generated by `validation/operational/ekf_storm_replay.jl` and
`validation/operational/ekf_inject_replay.jl`.
